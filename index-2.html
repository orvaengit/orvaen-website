Find Businesses That Need Websites

Find 15+ Indian businesses using publicly available business information, prioritising businesses with no website or a weak web presence.

Find 15+ potential business clients in India who could benefit from a new website. Prioritize legitimate businesses that: - do not appear to have an official website, or - have an outdated, broken, poorly designed, or ineffective website. Focus on businesses such as photographers, wedding studios, salons, restaurants, cafés, gyms, clinics, local brands, agencies, stores and other service businesses. For each business, provide only publicly available business information: 1. Business name 2. City 3. Business category 4. Publicly listed phone number, if available 5. Publicly listed business email, if available 6. Instagram or other official social profile, if available 7. Website URL, if they have one 8. A short explanation of why their current web presence could be improved Aim for approximately 80% businesses without an official website and 20% with a clearly weak/outdated website. Search broadly and prioritize different businesses on each run. Avoid repeating businesses already found when possible. Do not provide private, leaked, or non-public contact information. Return the results in a clean table suitable for legitimate business outreach.
